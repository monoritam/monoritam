Drums tracking service

Whether prefer to record your own drums in our studio or have us do it for you, we have the old-school equipment to deliver tasty results. Our microphones, vintage Soundtracs analogue mixer, RME soundcard, and 16-channel drum tracks will give you the sound you're looking for.
